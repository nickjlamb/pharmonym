How it works

Enter any brand or generic drug name to see its equivalent, drug classification, common uses, typical dosage, side effects, and safety warnings.

"At a glance" summaries are AI-generated from official prescribing information (FDA / UK SmPC) and condense the full label text shown beneath each card, with source links. Not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always verify with the full prescribing information or your pharmacist.

Pharmonym runs on the same open-source engine as PubCrawl — our MCP server that connects AI to biomedical literature, drug labels, and clinical-trial data.
